## Global Bebas Neue subtitle style (applied to all 10 clips)

- **Font:** Bebas Neue, ALL CAPS (burned in by the clipper)
- **Weight/size:** Bold, ~70–80 pt at 1080×1920 (roughly 1/12 of frame height)
- **Placement:** Centered, lower third — baseline at ~75% frame height, safely above the YouTube Shorts UI (like/comment rail and title zone)
- **Color:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` with 6–8 px black stroke + subtle drop shadow (40% opacity, 4 px offset)
- **Emphasis words:** Highlight one power word per line in Vice City pink `#FF3EA5` or cyan `#3EE6FF` (e.g., "CHECKS", "PRISON", "GUNS")
- **Chunking:** 1–3 words per card, word-by-word pop-in (5% scale punch on each word), no line ever longer than 60% of frame width
- **Timing:** Subtitle card changes on every beat/cut; never let a card sit longer than 1.2 s
