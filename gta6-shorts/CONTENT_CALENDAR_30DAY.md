## FACELESS PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Voice:** one consistent AI/recorded voice = channel identity. Higgsfield `create_voice` + `generate_audio` can produce the narration from the scripts in `GTA6_SHORTS_PLAN.md` when credits allow.
- **Batching:** produce Week 2's 🛠️ edits on Day 8, Week 3's on Day 15, etc. One editing day per week.
- **Copyright reality:** trailer footage is widely re-used in this niche, but monetization can be limited on pure re-uploads — the voiceover + editorial framing (theories, comparisons, breakdowns) is what makes it transformative. Long-forms are the monetization engine; Shorts are the growth engine.
- **Series branding:** recurring intro stingers per series ("Details You Missed" / "Theory Thursday" / "Real or Rockstar") so faceless still feels like a show.
- **Measure:** after Week 1, double down on whichever Short style (quote clips vs stunt clips vs theory clips) holds retention past 70%.
